Discover Rishikesh

Rishikesh lies in the Himalayan foothills beside the sacred River Ganga. Known for its peaceful ashrams, yoga centres, riverfront ghats and green surroundings, it offers a memorable blend of spirituality, culture and outdoor experiences for visitors seeking relaxation and adventure.

“Where the river brings peace and every journey begins within.”

Best Time to Visit

Spring (Mar - Apr)

Pleasant weather for riverside walks and sightseeing.

Summer (May - Jun)

Warm days; explore early mornings and shaded places.

Monsoon (Jul - Sep)

Lush green surroundings, but heavy rain may affect travel.

Winter (Oct - Feb)

Cooler weather, ideal for yoga, temples and sightseeing.

How to Reach

By Flight

Nearest airport: Jolly Grant, Dehradun (approx. 21 km)

By Train

Rishikesh station; Haridwar Junction is another option.

By Car

Road links from Delhi, Haridwar and Dehradun; time varies.

By Bike

A scenic journey from nearby cities; check weather and road conditions.

Important Travel Information

Permits

Weather

Connectivity

Health Care

Packing Guide

Walking shoes, sun protection, rainwear and warm layers.

Responsible Tourism

Respect local customs, avoid littering and protect the Ganga.
