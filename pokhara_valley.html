Discover Pokhara Valley

Pokhara Valley is a scenic destination in Nepal, known for its peaceful lakes, green hills, and stunning views of the Annapurna range. With lakeside promenades, caves, waterfalls, and lively local culture, it offers a memorable escape for nature lovers and adventure seekers.

“Where tranquil lakes reflect the beauty of the Himalayas.”

Best Time to Visit

Spring (Mar - May)

Pleasant days, green hills and clear mountain views.

Monsoon (Jun - Aug)

Warm, rainy weather; check forecasts before outdoor plans.

Autumn (Sep - Nov)

Clearer skies and beautiful Annapurna views, ideal for photos.

Winter (Dec - Feb)

Cooler days and chilly mornings; city winters are usually mild.

How to Reach

By Flight

Pokhara International Airport; check current flight routes.

By Train

No direct train to Pokhara; continue by road from a rail hub.

By Car

Road connections from Kathmandu; journey time varies with traffic.

By Bike

Scenic routes for experienced riders; follow local road-safety rules.
