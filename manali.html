Discover Manali

Manali is a beautiful mountain town in the Kullu Valley, known for the Beas River, deodar forests, ancient temples and snow-capped peaks. With charming villages, lively markets and scenic mountain views, it offers a memorable escape for nature lovers, culture enthusiasts and adventure seekers.

“Where the river flows gently and the mountains welcome you.”

Best Time to Visit

Spring (Mar - May)

Pleasant weather, blossoming orchards and fresh mountain air.

Summer (Jun - Aug)

Warm days, green valleys and plenty of sightseeing options.

Autumn (Sep - Nov)

Clear skies, golden forests, ideal for photography.

Winter (Dec - Feb)

Cold days and snowy nearby slopes; check road conditions.

How to Reach

By Flight

Nearest airport: Bhuntar (approx. 50 km from Manali).

By Train

Rail options: Joginder Nagar or Pathankot; continue by road.

By Car

Via Kullu on NH3; Delhi is about 530 km away by road.

By Bike

A scenic mountain ride; check weather and road conditions.
