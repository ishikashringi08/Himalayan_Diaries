Discover Gulmarg

Gulmarg, meaning “Meadow of Flowers,” is a beautiful hill station in Jammu and Kashmir, known for its green meadows, pine forests, snowy mountains, and the famous Gulmarg Gondola. In summer, colourful flowers brighten the landscape, while winter brings snow and opportunities for winter sports. It is a wonderful destination for nature lovers and adventure enthusiasts.

“Where every meadow blooms and every mountain tells a story.”

Best Time to Visit

Spring (Mar - May)

Mild weather, blooming meadows and pleasant walks.

Summer (Jun - Aug)

Green landscapes, pleasant days, ideal for sightseeing.

Autumn (Sep - Oct)

Crisp air, golden colours and beautiful mountain views.

Winter (Dec - Feb)

Snowy slopes and winter sports when conditions permit.

How to Reach

By Flight

Nearest airport: Srinagar (approx. 56 km)

By Train

Nearest railhead: Baramulla (approx. 53 km)

By Car

Via Srinagar and Tangmarg (about 2 hrs from Srinagar)

By Bike

A scenic mountain ride; check snow and road conditions first.
