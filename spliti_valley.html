Discover Spiti Valley

Spiti Valley is a cold desert in the Himalayas, known for its breathtaking landscapes, ancient monasteries, and unique Tibetan culture. Surrounded by snow-capped peaks and rugged mountains, it offers a peaceful escape for nature lovers, adventure seekers and culture enthusiasts.

“A place where time slows down and nature speaks louder.”

Best Time to Visit

Spring (Mar - May)

Mild weather, blooming landscapes, fewer crowds.

Summner (Jun - Aug)

Pleasant days, great for travel and sightseeing.

Autumn (Sep - Oct)

Clear sides, beautiful colors, ideal for photography.

Winter (Nov - Feb)

Very cold, snow-covered (Only for Experienced travelers)

How to Reach

By Flight

Nearest airport: Kullu (Bhuntar) (approx. 250km)

By Train

Nearest Station: Jammu Tewi (approx. 330km)

By Car

Via Shimla/Manali (approx. 12 - 16hrs depending on route)

By Bike

Best for adventure lovers. Plan for rough roads & winter conditions.
