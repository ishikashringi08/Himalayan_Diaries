Discover Pelling

Pelling is a peaceful hill town in Sikkim, known for breathtaking views of the Kanchenjunga range, green valleys, waterfalls, and Buddhist monasteries. With historic ruins, scenic viewpoints, forested hills, and welcoming local culture, it offers a memorable escape for nature lovers and curious travellers.

“Where the mountains touch the clouds and every view inspires wonder.”

Best Time to Visit

Spring (Mar - May)

Mild weather, blooming rhododendrons and fresh mountain views.

Monsoon (Jun - Sep)

Lush green landscapes, but heavy rain may affect roads and views.

Autumn (Sep - Nov)

Clearer skies and fine Kanchenjunga views, ideal for photos.

Winter (Dec - Feb)

Chilly mornings and occasional snow nearby; mountain views depend on weather.

How to Reach

By Flight

Bagdogra Airport is a common gateway; continue to Pelling by road.

By Train

Nearest major railway station: New Jalpaiguri (NJP); continue by road.

By Car

Road connections from Siliguri and Gangtok; journey time varies by route.

By Bike

Scenic mountain roads for experienced riders; check weather and road conditions.
