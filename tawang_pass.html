Discover Spiti Valley

SSela Pass is a spectacular high-altitude mountain pass in Arunachal Pradesh, known for its snow-covered peaks, breathtaking Himalayan landscapes and serene alpine lake. Serving as a gateway to Tawang, it offers travellers an unforgettable journey through rugged mountains and beautiful natural scenery.

“Where the mountains wear snow, and silence feels infinite.”

Best Time to Visit

Spring (Mar - June)

Mild weather, blooming landscapes, fewer crowds.

Monsoon (July - Sep)

Rainfall can affect road conditions and visibility. Landslides and travel disruptions are possible.

Autumn (Sep - Nov)

Crisp mountain air and beautiful Himalayan scenery make this a popular period for photography and sightseeing.

Winter (Dec - Feb)

Snow-covered landscapes and frozen lakes create beautiful winter views. However, extreme cold and snowfall can temporarily close mountain roads.

How to Reach

By Flight

Nearest airport: Lokpriya Gopinath Bordoloi International Airport in Guwahati.

By Train

Nearest Station: Guwahati is a major railway gateway.

By Car

Bhalukpong, Bomdila, Dirang

By Bike

The region has scenic mountain roads and impressive landscapes.

Culture & Traditions

1. The Monpa community has a rich cultural heritage in the Tawang region.

2. Losar celebrates the traditional Tibetan New Year.

3. The Torgya festival features sacred masked dances at Tawang Monastery.

Important Travel Information

Permits

Indian nationals need an Inner Line Permit (ILP) to enter Arunachal Pradesh.

Weather

Snowfall, heavy rain and rapidly changing weather can affect traffic through Sela Pass.

Connectivity

Mobile connectivity may vary along mountain roads and in remote areas. Save important information and accommodation details before travelling.
